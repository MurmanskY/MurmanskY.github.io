# About

Hi 👋 I'm a Ph.D. student in the integrated M.S.–Ph.D. program at Fudan University, working with the NISL Lab. Before that, I got my B.Eng. in Computer Science (Excellence Program) from Nankai University, and spent a year as a research intern at Tsinghua's Mengshi Lab.
